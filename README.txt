Wendel Lacson | Submitted to Dr. Adrianne Brent S. Castro

HOW TO OPEN
  Double-click index.html. It opens in your browser.
  (Fonts load from the internet; everything else works offline.)

FOLDER CONTENTS
  index.html      The page itself (intro and final destination text)
  css/style.css   Colours, glass panels, cursor, screenshot viewer
  js/main.js      Chapters, prompt cards, 3D scene, rocket and landing
  js/three.min.js The 3D library (three.js r128)
  screenshots/    Compressed copies of the AI chat screenshots shown on the cards
                  (originals stay in the "AI Chatbot" folder)

THE STORY (8 chapters)

  4 The Broken Bot Labyrinth ... Aug 28, Game 1

  7 Teaching the AI Who I Am ... after PDC10, personalization
  8 Prompting with Purpose ..... after PDC10, Nov 2026

WHERE TO EDIT CONTENT
  Open js/main.js and find "const CHAPTERS". Each chapter has a title,
  date label, story, what I learned, and its prompt cards. A card's
  "shots" list names screenshot files in the screenshots folder.
